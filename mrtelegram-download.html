FEATURES

Everything the settings screens give you

Comprehensive overview of customizations, privacy controls, and iOS UI enhancements in MRTelegram.

iOS interface style

Switch between Telegram (default) and iOS fluid physics. Screens not yet adapted keep the official look.

App font

Pick iOS, SF Pro Text, Samsung One UI, Linotte, Rubrik, Expletus Sans or your system font.

Faster downloads and uploads

Transfers files in parallel, with a download acceleration level you choose.

Home screen

Hide the All folder or search bar, change folder style, and show two or three lines per chat.

Hide read checks in channels

Removes sent and read checks in channels only. Chats and groups stay normal.

Conversation screen

iOS-style header, input bar, search circle and context menu, plus translation and system emojis.

Stickers and bubbles

Resize stickers with a live preview and choose your bubble style.

Voice messages

Reduces noise and echo and evens out volume while you record.

View deleted messages

Keeps messages deleted after you received them and marks them as deleted on your phone.

Hide phone number

Hides your number inside the MRTelegram interface. Telegram privacy settings do not change.

Local Premium

Treats your account as Premium inside the app, so interface premium features work without a subscription.

Notification preferences

Permanent notification and a status list for permission, push service and battery optimization.

Ready to install

One file, installed like any other Android app. Log in with your Telegram account.
